# Prime Ticket Customer Guide

## Submit a support request

1. Sign in to your organization's Frappe or ERPNext site.
2. Open **Submit a Ticket** from the Prime Ticket workspace, or visit `/new-ticket`.
3. Enter a short subject that describes the issue.
4. Choose the closest category and priority. Select **Urgent** only when the issue needs immediate attention.
5. Describe what happened, what you expected, and any steps that help reproduce the issue.
6. Select **Send Request**.

After submission, Prime Ticket confirms that the request was received. The support team will follow up by email when the status changes. Keep the subject concise and avoid including passwords, API keys, or other secrets in the description.

## What happens next

- Your request is saved as a Support Ticket and starts with the **Open** status.
- You can see tickets your account has permission to access.
- The support team may update the status, ask for more information, or record a resolution.
- If an email says the ticket is waiting for your reply, sign in and provide the requested details.

## If the form does not submit

Check that all required fields are filled in and that you are signed in. If the problem continues, contact your organization's support team through its usual channel.
